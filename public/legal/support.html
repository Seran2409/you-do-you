Help and contact

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to [E-Mail-Adresse]. We'll answer as soon as we can.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. There is no account and no server, so nobody but you sees your lists.

How do I back up my data or move it to a new phone?

Settings → “Export data” creates a file. On the new device: Settings → “Import data” and pick the file. Importing replaces the data on the new device.

Reminders don't arrive.

Check in the app's settings that “Morning reminder” is on, and in your phone's system settings that the app may send notifications. Some Android phones delay reminders to save battery; exclude the app from battery optimization there. Open the app now and then so it can plan the next days' reminders.

Why doesn't the app turn on Do Not Disturb?

On Android the app needs your permission once: Meditate → “Do not disturb” → “Allow access”. On iPhone, Apple doesn't allow apps to do this; turn on the Do Not Disturb focus in Control Center before you meditate.

How do XP and levels work?

Every finished to-do, subtask, routine check-in and minute of meditation earns XP. Hard to-dos count more, reached goals give bonus XP. The full table is in the app's “Level” tab.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → “Delete all data”, or uninstall the app.
